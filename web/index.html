Pallanguzhi · பல்லாங்குழி, against a Go AI

The fourteen-pit mancala game played across Tamil Nadu. You play the bottom row. Lift all seeds from one of your pits and sow them one by one, counter-clockwise. The rules engine and the opponent are Go, compiled to WebAssembly and running in your tab.

Rules

  • After your last seed, if the next pit has seeds, you lift them and keep sowing.
  • If the next pit is empty, you capture everything in the pit after it, and your turn ends.
  • Pasu: any pit you bring to exactly 6 seeds is yours at once.
  • When the player to move has no seeds, the round ends; each side keeps the seeds on its row. Most seeds wins.
Moving first is a disadvantage in this version: between two random players the second player wins 70% of games.
